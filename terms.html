  • FCFB is free. There is no real-money play, entry fees, or prizes, and FCFB may not be used for betting or gambling.

Community rules

When you chat or name your team, don't post:

  • Harassment, bullying, threats, or anything telling someone to hurt themselves.
  • Hate speech or slurs about anyone's race, ethnicity, religion, gender, sexual orientation, disability, or other identity.
  • Sexual content, graphic violence, or anything illegal.
  • Spam, scams, or pretending to be someone else.
  • Other people's private information.

There is zero tolerance for objectionable content or abusive users. FCFB filters offensive words, and you can report any message or block any person right from the chat. Reports are reviewed, and we may remove content or suspend or delete accounts that break these rules, without warning.
